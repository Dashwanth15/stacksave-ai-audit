## FAQ — 5 Real Q&As

**Q: Is this actually free? What's the catch?**
A: Completely free. No credit card, no account. StackSave is a lead-generation tool for Credex, which sells discounted AI infrastructure credits. We surface real savings, and for users with significant spend, we introduce Credex as an option. You're under no obligation.

---

**Q: How accurate are the savings estimates?**
A: All pricing data comes directly from official vendor pricing pages, verified as of the submission date and documented in our open-source repository. The audit logic is deterministic — the same inputs always produce the same outputs. We don't invent savings; if your stack is already optimized, we'll tell you that honestly.

---

**Q: Do I need to create an account or log in?**
A: No login required to run an audit. We only ask for your email after you've seen your results, and only if you want us to save the report and notify you of future changes.

---

**Q: What happens to my data?**
A: Your audit results are stored to generate your shareable URL. The public share version strips your company name and email — only tool names and savings amounts are shown. We don't sell your data. Your email is used only for your audit confirmation and, for high-savings cases, a note about Credex.

---

**Q: What if I'm already spending efficiently?**
A: We'll tell you that. Honestly. "You're spending well" is a valid audit result. We don't manufacture savings to look useful — that would make the tool worthless. If your stack is optimal, the audit confirms it and offers to notify you if pricing changes affect your tools.
