## Who This Is For

StackSave is useful if any of these sound familiar:

- You're paying for 3+ AI tools and couldn't confidently explain why each one is necessary
- Someone on your team signed up for a plan and you're not sure if you're still using it
- Your AI tool spend has grown month over month without a clear decision driving it
- You've never checked whether annual billing would be cheaper for the tools you use daily
- You're a solo founder or small team and "AI subscriptions" just get paid without review

If your stack is two tools and you know exactly what each costs and why — you probably don't need this. If it's messier than that, the audit takes 2 minutes.

## FAQ — 5 Real Q&As

**Q: Is this actually free? What's the catch?**
A: Completely free. No credit card, no account. StackSave is a lead-generation tool for Credex, which sells discounted AI infrastructure credits. We surface real savings, and for users with significant API spend, we introduce Credex as one option. You're under no obligation to do anything with that.

---

**Q: How accurate are the savings estimates?**
A: All pricing data comes directly from official vendor pricing pages, verified manually and documented in the repository. The audit logic is a deterministic rule engine — not AI-generated. The same inputs always produce the same outputs. Seven rules check for overpaid plans, unused seats, overlapping tools, annual billing savings, and API spend optimization. The AI (Grok) only writes the prose summary paragraph — it never touches the numbers. If your stack is already optimized, the audit says that. We don't inflate savings to look useful.

---

**Q: Do I need to create an account or log in?**
A: No login required to run an audit. We ask for your email after you've seen your results — not before. If you'd rather not share it, the results page stays accessible for the duration of your session.

---

**Q: What happens to my data?**
A: Your audit results are stored to generate a shareable URL and so you can retrieve them later. The public share version strips your company name and email — only tool names, plan details, and savings amounts are visible. We don't sell your data. Your email is used for the audit confirmation and, if your potential savings are above a threshold, a single note about Credex. No drip campaigns, no third-party sharing.

---

**Q: What if I'm already spending efficiently?**
A: We'll tell you that. Honestly. "Your stack looks well-optimized" is a valid and common audit result. We don't manufacture savings to appear useful — that would make the tool worthless the second someone fact-checks a recommendation. If your stack is optimal, the audit confirms it. If pricing changes later affect tools you use, you can re-run in a minute.
